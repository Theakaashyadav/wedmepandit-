Meaning of Important Hindu Wedding Rituals

Ganesh Puja

The wedding ceremony begins with Ganesh Puja for marriage, seeking blessings for a smooth and successful wedding celebration.

Jaimala Ceremony

The bride and groom exchange garlands in the famous Indian wedding varmala ceremony, symbolizing acceptance and respect for each other.

Kanyadaan Ritual

The emotional Kanyadaan ceremony represents the parents entrusting their daughter’s future to the groom with love and blessings.

Saat Phere and Vedic Mantras

The sacred Saat Phere in Hindu marriage are the heart of the wedding. Each phera represents promises of love, loyalty, trust, prosperity, and companionship.

Sindoor and Mangalsutra

These are powerful symbols of marriage and lifelong commitment in traditional Hindu culture.
