# 60-Day SEO Roadmap

## Days 1–10

Deploy and crawl the completed metadata, sitemap, heading, image-attribute, navigation, and schema fixes. Verify canonical responses on production. Submit the sitemap in Search Console.

## Days 11–20

Convert the three blog URLs to static/prerendered articles. Add breadcrumbs and accurate Article schema. Resolve every broken asset and route found by a production crawl.

## Days 21–35

Have the owner confirm services, coverage, address, claims, and ritual content. Improve the confirmed core service pages with reviewed process, preparation, FAQ, and related-service sections.

## Days 36–50

Publish no more than two reviewed articles per week. Add reciprocal internal links between articles and their single primary service intent. Avoid near-duplicate city or keyword pages.

## Days 51–60

Review Search Console indexing and queries, GA4 interaction events, production Core Web Vitals, conversions, and crawl errors. Refresh priorities from verified data; do not infer rankings or volume.
