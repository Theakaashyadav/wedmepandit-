# Website Design Improvements

Implemented a shared maroon/ivory/white/champagne-gold system in `phase3.css` and loaded it on 34 full HTML documents. It standardizes 1180px containers, responsive spacing, typography, pill CTAs, focus rings, cards, borders, shadows, breadcrumbs and reduced-motion behavior.

Main-page improvements include refined homepage sections/cards and hero captions, consistent About cards, premium blog cards, gallery framing/responsive columns, article borders/typography, and a cleaner Contact panel. Existing logo, photographs, slider, gallery and brand identity were preserved. No new framework, stock image or generated visual was introduced.

All 25 service documents now share refined hero/detail/card/CTA styling without replacing their existing content. Browser evidence: 170 page/viewport checks passed at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px with no horizontal overflow.
