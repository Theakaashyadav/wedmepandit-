# Services Page Optimization

`service.html` was rebuilt as the highest-priority Phase 3 page.

## Before

- Orange gradient outside the established brand.
- Seven plain stacked text cards.
- No service imagery or consistent actions.
- Unverified duration, price and arrangement details were emphasized.

## After

- Premium branded hero with breadcrumb and two CTAs.
- Clear H1: “Hindu Puja & Pandit Ji Services.”
- Ten crawlable cards, limited to existing pages: Vivah, Sthapna, Namkaran, 16 Sanskar, Janmdivas, Pravachan, Festival Pooja, Shanti Pooja, Havan and Yagya.
- Verified existing imagery with dimensions/lazy loading.
- Responsive 3/2/1-column grid.
- View Details and Enquire actions on each card.
- Introductory guidance, conservative trust section and final phone/WhatsApp CTA.
- Unique title, description and Open Graph metadata.
- `noindex, follow` retained pending owner approval.

All 10 card destinations passed browser checks. No new service offering was created.
